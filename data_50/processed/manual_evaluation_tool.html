Method
The authors created GSM1k, a new dataset of 1,205 grade-school arithmetic problems. To ensure comparability, they instructed annotators to write problems matching the difficulty distribution (number of steps) of GSM8k, and then filtered the generated problems to match GSM8k's answer magnitude distribution. They validated the dataset's similarity using a human distinguishability test where 19 annotators (uninvolved in creation) attempted to identify one GSM1k problem out of a set of five (four from GSM8k) across 1,205 attempts. Finally, they evaluated multiple LLMs (including the Claude and Llama families) on both datasets, measuring accuracy and sequence-level log-likelihood on the GSM8k test set to analyze overfitting and potential data leakage.

Method
The authors develop 'GSM-Ranges', a tool using Python templates to systematically perturb the numerical values of base problems from the GSM8K dataset. They define six levels of perturbation: Level 1 (same-digit replacement), Level 2 (100–1,000), Level 3 (1,000–10,000), Level 4 (10,000–100,000), Level 5 (100,000–1,000,000), and Level 6 (1,000,000–10,000,000). To dissect the failures, they track logical vs. non-logical error rates, measure mean token counts (specifically for o3-mini), and perform a manual error analysis on 100 random samples across Levels 4, 5, and 6 to detect 'Number-Copy Errors' and standalone arithmetic errors.

Method
The VoroNav framework executes zero-shot object navigation using a combination of Voronoi-based spatial representations and a Large Language Model for global decision-making, supported by a Remote Visual Grounding (RVG) module. The system constructs Voronoi diagrams from the agent's environmental observations to define navigational pathways and frontier nodes. At key global decision points, the LLM processes environmental descriptions and spatial context to select the most promising navigation targets or paths. The paper evaluates this through qualitative visualization of episodes and ablation studies comparing variants of the framework.
